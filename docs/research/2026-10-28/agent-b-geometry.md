# Agent B — electrode geometry and channel adaptation

Research audit: **28 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata**. Research only; no experiment, training, submission, or cloud resource was started. Measured agent token usage: **unavailable**.

The useful lesson is to preserve meaningful electrode identity while testing whether an adaptation removes nuisance variation or removes command information. Neither paper establishes a gain on PROTEUS. Both leads are verified; the adaptation benchmark remains a preprint, and its metric description has an unresolved internal inconsistency.

## Contract and comparison boundary

The current [official FAQ](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/neural-interfaces26/2026-competition/main/codabench/pages/scientific_faq_bci.md) supplies one chronological `(1,41,480)` EEG window at 120 Hz: four seconds, starting one second after the cue. Labels are motor imagery, mental subtraction, and word generation. Prediction receives no participant, session, interface, run, or label metadata. A fresh model copy and optional reset start every participant-session. Current/past-session label-free adaptation is permitted; state cannot cross sessions. Score is balanced accuracy averaged equally over participant × session × interface cells. Public data comprise ten people, fourteen sessions, and 112 runs; only two people have three sessions.

The [Terms, Rule 4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/neural-interfaces26/2026-competition/main/codabench/pages/terms.md) permit declared public external data subject to its terms. The user's fixed-corpus constraint is stricter: borrowing a design is compatible; importing either study's pretrained weights introduces external training data. No result below estimates competition gain. The read-only [research journal](C:/Projects/EEG-EMG-Foundation-Challenge-2026/eeg-emg-challenge-2026-track2-bcidecoding/docs/RESEARCH_JOURNAL.md) records linear cell BA 0.379661 and EEGNet seeds 33/34/35 at 0.352390/0.382836/0.363921 on one participant split, not three folds.

## REVE

**Citation/status.** Yassine El Ouahidi, Jonathan Lys, Philipp Thölke, Nicolas Farrugia, Bastien Pasdeloup, Vincent Gripon, Karim Jerbi, and Giulia Lioi. *REVE: A Foundation Model for EEG—Adapting to Any Setup with Large-Scale Pretraining on 25,000 Subjects*. NeurIPS 2025; [arXiv:2510.21585](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21585), submitted 24 October 2025; verified [conference paper, including appendices](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/20a917f77773ac0fa8bea2bdd6606b66-Paper-Conference.pdf).

**Authors demonstrated — paper evidence capsule.** Raw-EEG masked reconstruction trains a transformer with `(x,y,z,patch-time)` Fourier/learned positional features, coordinate noise, and spatial/temporal block masking. Ten downstream datasets cover MI, speech imagery, arithmetic/stress, emotion, sleep, abnormalities, and events. Relevant populations include PhysioNet-MI 109, BCIC-IV-2a nine, arithmetic 36, and speech fifteen participants. PhysioNet/arithmetic split by participant; speech splits trials within participants. Evaluation includes supervised fine-tuning and frozen-encoder probes, not label-free deployment. Table 19's learned-position variant nearly matches the coordinate method on two tasks; removing position noise reduces their reported average. Sparse-channel results are task dependent: MI degrades more gradually than speech. Bipolar TUEV uses electrode-pair midpoint coordinates. Few-shot evaluation requires labeled target examples; its cross-dataset variant additionally uses labeled MI corpora. Pretraining normalizes using recording-session statistics and excludes downstream datasets; BCIC preprocessing is described as Euclidean alignment. These are not chronological target-statistic guarantees. ([Paper §§2–3, Appendix C–E](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/20a917f77773ac0fa8bea2bdd6606b66-Paper-Conference.pdf))

**Release audit.** The [official project](https://brain-bzh.github.io/reve/) reports approximately 60,000 hours from 92 datasets and 25,000 people, releases weights, and specifies 200-Hz input. Its March 2026 code/data release does not imply that every upstream corpus is unrestricted. Repository HEAD inspected: [`06a7059a07c3dabd80aee60c3dbc1eca4bdbe1c7`](https://github.com/elouayas/reve_eeg/tree/06a7059a07c3dabd80aee60c3dbc1eca4bdbe1c7), dated 20 March 2026 UTC.

The pinned [encoder](https://github.com/elouayas/reve_eeg/blob/06a7059a07c3dabd80aee60c3dbc1eca4bdbe1c7/src/models/encoder.py) confirms shared per-channel patch embedding, training-only coordinate noise, and patch-index time. Its [position utility](https://github.com/elouayas/reve_eeg/blob/06a7059a07c3dabd80aee60c3dbc1eca4bdbe1c7/src/downstream_tasks/position_utils.py) resolves electrode names or position files and averages bipolar endpoints. The released [BCIC preprocessor](https://github.com/elouayas/reve_eeg/blob/06a7059a07c3dabd80aee60c3dbc1eca4bdbe1c7/preprocessing/preprocessing_bciciv2a.py) splits subjects 1–5/6–7/8–9, common-average references, filters at 0.3–50 Hz, and resamples four seconds to 800 samples. It contains no Euclidean alignment, contrary to the paper's 0.5–99.5-Hz-plus-alignment description. The inspected release therefore does not fully resolve the reported protocol.

**Our interpretation.** Coordinates retain *where* a measured voltage belongs; temporal patch indices retain ordering inside the supplied window. Accepting a new layout does not make arbitrary electrode exchange harmless: swapping voltages without matching coordinates changes the scientific input. Coordinate jitter changes metadata while leaving voltages fixed; real movement also changes volume-conducted mixtures, contact, and artifacts. Their equivalence is untested. A bipolar difference is not a monopolar voltage measured at its midpoint. Successful classification under that convention is empirical compatibility, not an exact forward model.

Noncausal attention within the already-delivered four-second window is compatible with the competition contract. Whole-future-session normalization is not. Upsampling 120-Hz competition data to a pretrained model's rate cannot restore missing bandwidth. The learned-position ablation also prevents crediting geometry alone for the large model's results: corpus, objective, architecture, and adaptation differ together.

**Untested PROTEUS component and falsifier.** Borrow only coordinate-defined neighborhood masking: mask a small contiguous scalp neighborhood and time interval during otherwise unchanged same-corpus training. Compare equal masking budgets for geometric versus random channel groups and no masking. Keep channel identity at inference. Falsify the geometry rationale if random groups perform equally or better across held-out participants, or masking suppresses one command despite a pooled improvement. This tests a small spatial prior, not a reproduction of REVE.

## Channel Adaptation for EEG Foundation Models

**Citation/status.** Kuntal Kokate, Bruno Aristimunha, Dung Truong, and Arnaud Delorme. *Channel Adaptation for EEG Foundation Models: A Systematic Benchmark Across Architectures, Tasks, and Training Regimes*. [arXiv:2604.23091v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23091), 25 April 2026. Verified six-page [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.23091v1) and [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23091v1); no peer-reviewed venue verified.

**Authors demonstrated — paper evidence capsule.** The benchmark compares learned channel projection, coordinate-based spherical-spline interpolation, 25 spherical-harmonic coefficients, and SSI followed by per-subject geometric-mean covariance whitening. Five externally pretrained backbones—BENDR, Neuro-GPT, EEGPT, LUNA, CBraMod—are tested with frozen probes and supervised full fine-tuning over 10–15 seeds. BCIC2A has nine participants split 1–3/4–6/7–9; PhysioNet has 109 split 70/19/20; FACED has 123 split 80/20/23; MDD has 64 with predefined subject splits; TUEV uses its official recording split. Tasks cover MI, emotion, depression, and events. Rigid inputs require adaptation; flexible backbones can lose performance with external adaptation during fine-tuning. Reported CBraMod/TUEV whitening changes from 39.4% probe to 16.7% fine-tuning. Source harmonics perform poorly on MI despite useful depression results. Whitening's target-data chronology is unspecified; supervised heads/projections require training labels. Reported seed-level significance does not establish participant-level uncertainty. ([Methods–limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23091v1))

**Audit gaps and our interpretation.** No benchmark repository, complete seed tables, supplement, or covariance-fitting implementation was linked in the inspected paper/arXiv page or located by targeted search. Consequently I cannot verify whether target whitening uses all a held-out person's windows, how validation is constructed for TUEV, or whether the declared metric is implemented consistently. The paper calls its outcome macro accuracy/equivalent balanced accuracy, yet explains a constant-class FACED result of 12.5% by class imbalance. **If all nine classes occur**, a constant classifier's balanced accuracy is exactly 1/9, independent of class prevalence. That arithmetic makes precise numerical comparisons provisional, without proving every result wrong.

Interpolation preserves a smooth scalp-field approximation, not information absent from the original sensors. Learned projection preserves whatever labeled optimization finds useful; it supplies no anatomical guarantee. Low-order harmonics emphasize coarse spatial patterns, but are not identified cortical sources. The [OmnEEG dependency README](https://github.com/brain2vec/OmnEEG) separately marks anatomical source reconstruction as unfinished. Whitening changes covariance relative to an estimated reference; an invertible map can retain signal information mathematically while still making a frozen decoder ineffective. Conflicting gradients and focal-versus-global explanations in the discussion remain hypotheses, not mechanistic interventions. Neither deep spatial filters nor attention weights identify neural generators.

**Untested PROTEUS component and falsifier.** Test shrinkage covariance normalization on the existing 41 channels, without forcing an unnecessary 19-channel montage or importing weights. Fit source references on training people only. Compare frozen inference with a current/past-window session reference initialized/reset identically; include the current window only if consistently specified. No labels, IDs, future windows, or previous-session statistics enter prediction. Replay chronology and score equal cells. Falsify usefulness if gains disappear under causal replay, fail across held-out participants, or trade improved average performance for repeated command collapse. Test encoder training regime separately; the paper does not authorize assuming normalization helps EEGNet.
